SpeciesMap

Add two or more species to see where their ranges overlap. Add a genus or family and the app combines the ranges of its 40 most-observed species; you can then show those species separately.

About the ranges. They are predictions from iNaturalist's geomodel, built from where people have recorded each species. They are estimates, not surveyed boundaries, and less-observed species may have no range at all.

About sightings. The Sightings button adds a dot for each research-grade iNaturalist observation. Dots show where people have looked, so gaps do not mean absence. Faint dots are sightings whose location iNaturalist has blurred, usually to protect a threatened species.
